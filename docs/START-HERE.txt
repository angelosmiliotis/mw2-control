MW2 Control 1.1

Open the Standalone EXE and start MW2 (2009) Multiplayer through Steam.
The .NET runtime is included. Wait for Connected, choose your settings,
then click Apply. Leave MW2 Control open while you play.

Your choices are remembered. Apply keeps them active and reapplies
recognized game resets in the same game session. Click Apply again
after restarting the game. Reset stops this and restores original
values where they can still be verified.

Lobby Network needs Npcap: https://npcap.com/#download
Join a lobby and click Scan lobby. The likely host is an estimate;
Steam relays can affect the result. Block and Unblock ask for Windows
administrator access when needed. Blocks remain until you remove them.
