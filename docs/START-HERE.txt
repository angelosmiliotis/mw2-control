MW2 Control 1.0

Extract the entire ZIP, then open MW2Control.exe.
The Windows x64 download includes the .NET 8 Desktop Runtime.
Keep the extracted files together.

Start MW2 (2009) Multiplayer through Steam. Pick your settings and click
Apply. Your choices are saved when you Apply or close the app.

The app detects the running game and keeps your applied settings active.
It retries recognized resets while the same game process is running.
Click Apply again after restarting the game. Reset stops automatic
recovery and restores original values where they can still be verified.

Lobby Network needs Npcap from https://npcap.com/#download.
Join a lobby, then click Scan lobby. Open MW2 Control as administrator
when you want to block or unblock an IP. The likely host is an estimate,
and Steam relays can affect the result.
