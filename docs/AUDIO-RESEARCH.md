# Notes on separate audio controls

Stream mute is the audio control included in MW2 Control. Separate sliders for music, ambience or round sounds would need their own verified mappings and in-game checks.

An earlier alias probe suggested music channel 13. That result has not been independently verified for the supported executable, and sound aliases can move as maps load. It is insufficient evidence for an additional control.

Any future audio option needs testing with footsteps, player gunfire, explosions, round transitions and the bomb timer on several maps. The existing stream-mute test is documented in [Audio](AUDIO-FINDINGS.md).
